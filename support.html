Frequently Asked Questions

How do I reset my password?
Open the app, go to Settings → tap "Change Password" and follow the steps. If you're signed out and can't get back in, email us and we'll help you regain access.

How do I delete my account and data?
Email us at the address above with the subject "Delete my account" from the email linked to your account, and we'll permanently remove your data within a few business days.

Why does the app ask for camera or photo access?
Camera access is used only when you choose to snap a photo of your meal for AI-based calorie estimation. Photo library access is used only if you pick an existing photo instead. Nothing is captured without your action.

Is my health data private?
Yes. Your data is tied to your account only and is never sold. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

For anything else — feedback, feature requests, or a bug report — just send us an email. We read every message.
